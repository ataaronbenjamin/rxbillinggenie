Behind the estimate

A planning tool for the potential value of pharmacy time and medication inventory.

How is the estimate calculated?

Time lost = monthly rejections × minutes per rejection ÷ 60.

Staff time cost = time lost × hourly staff cost.

Estimated recoverable staff time value = staff time cost × assumed time recovery percentage.

Estimated recoverable medication value = annual purchase-cost value at risk × estimated recoverable share. This is potential recovery, not confirmed savings.

Estimated combined net value = staff time value + medication value − transfer costs − subscription cost. The subscription is deducted only once.

Daily frequency uses your operating days per month. Weekly frequency uses 52 weeks ÷ 12 months. Annual values equal 12 monthly values. An annual subscription is spread over 12 months for comparison.

Where do the starting assumptions come from?

The defaults are a starting scenario, not a claim about your pharmacy. The 50% time recovery figure is an assumption for modelling and nothing more — move the slider to whatever you believe is realistic.

No medication recovery is assumed at all until you enter your own figures. Both medication fields start at zero on purpose.

Every input is yours to change. Nothing here is a guarantee of savings.

What does the calculator include?

Break-even minutes are the staff time you would need to recover each month to cover the remaining modelled costs after estimated medication recovery. Transfer costs increase those costs. If estimated medication recovery already covers them, no additional minutes are needed.

Not included: tax, training time, hardware, or any change in dispensing volume.

Planning estimates only, in Canadian dollars. Figures depend entirely on the inputs you provide and are not a forecast, a guarantee, or financial advice.
