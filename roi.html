What do the CPhA comparisons mean?

CPhA’s September 4, 2025 release describes a national survey of more than 1,100 pharmacists. Three quarters report at least five hours each week managing third-party payer claims; close to one fifth report more than fifteen hours weekly. Read the CPhA release.

The comparison converts weekly time to an average month using 52 ÷ 12. Five hours/week is approximately 21.7 hours/month; fifteen hours/week is 65 hours/month. A four-week period would instead be 20 and 60 hours. Both the national comparisons and your pharmacy’s staff time cost use your editable hourly cost. Survey time covers broader work than rejection troubleshooting, so it is not automatically counted as time Rx Billing Genie will recover.

How is the estimate calculated?

Time lost: monthly rejections × minutes per rejection ÷ 60.

Staff time cost: time lost × hourly staff cost.

Estimated recoverable staff time value: staff time cost × assumed time recovery percentage.

Estimated recoverable medication value: annual purchase-cost value at risk × estimated recoverable share. This is potential recovery, not confirmed savings.

Estimated combined net value: staff time value + medication value − transfer costs − subscription cost. The subscription is deducted only once.

Daily frequency uses your operating days per month. Weekly frequency uses 52 weeks ÷ 12 months. Annual values equal 12 monthly values. An annual subscription is spread over 12 months for comparison.

Break-even minutes are the recovered staff time needed to cover remaining modelled costs after estimated medication recovery. Transfer costs increase those costs. If estimated medication recovery already covers the costs, zero additional minutes are needed in the scenario. Equivalent rejections include your assumed recovery percentage and round up to a whole rejection. Per-person daily cost uses 365 calendar days. Figures are calculated before rounding.

Where do the starting assumptions come from?

The 13-minute added delay per rejection is an approximate weighted estimate from Rx Billing Genie’s 2025 pharmacist survey (approximately 70–75 respondents). The $35/hour blended staff cost and three rejections per day are established Rx Billing Genie planning inputs.

A four-person team and 30 operating days per month create an editable example. Medication value at risk, recoverable share and transfer costs start at zero; enter pharmacy-specific estimates. The 50% time recovery rate is an illustrative assumption, not a measured product outcome. The subscription baseline is the standard $49.99/month team plan, equivalent to $599.88/year. Team limits apply; use the custom-price field for your actual quoted total.

What does the calculator include?

This models the value of staff time potentially recovered with Rx Billing Genie and estimated recoverable medication value with Rx MedMatch. It is an estimate, not a guarantee of reduced payroll, increased revenue, or clinical outcomes. Medication transfer costs are included when entered. Taxes, training time and other implementation costs are excluded.

Rx MedMatch connects pharmacies with surplus stock and medication needs. Medication recovery is an editable estimate; stock listed or transferred does not automatically equal the amount recovered. Patient access benefits are not assigned a dollar value here.

Inputs are processed in your browser. This calculator does not send or store your pharmacy entries.
